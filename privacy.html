Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026 • Version 2.0

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

MailMate's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We specifically guarantee that Google workspace data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train generalized AI models, and never viewed by humans without explicit consent.

1. Introduction

MailMate ("we", "our", or "us") provides an intelligent email productivity assistant designed to help users manage their inboxes, identify key action items, organize deadlines, and draft replies safely. We take your privacy and personal data security with the utmost seriousness.

This Privacy Policy explains how MailMate collects, processes, and protects your information when you use our web application and connect your Google account via OAuth 2.0.

2. Google User Data We Access

When you connect MailMate with your Google account, we request explicit, granular permissions through Google OAuth. We only access the data required to provide email intelligence and scheduling features:

  • .../auth/gmail.readonly & .../auth/gmail.modify: Used to retrieve recent email headers, senders, dates, snippets, read states, and labels so you can view, triage, and organize your inbox.
  • .../auth/gmail.compose: Used to prepare and save email drafts that you can review, edit, and choose to send.
  • .../auth/calendar.events: Used to detect deadlines mentioned in your correspondence and synchronize them to your Google Calendar at your direction.
  • .../auth/userinfo.email & .../auth/userinfo.profile: Used to identify your account session and display your avatar and name in the workspace.

3. Ephemeral, In-Memory Processing

MailMate implements an ephemeral, zero-hoarding architecture for your raw mailbox content:

  • Volatile RAM Only: Raw email bodies, full message trees, and email attachments are retrieved directly from the Gmail API into transient process RAM while you are actively using MailMate.
  • No Raw Mail Storage: We do NOT write, store, or copy raw email contents, full bodies, or personal correspondence onto permanent database tables or public storage buckets.
  • Authenticated Encryption at Rest: Minimal operational state (such as OAuth refresh tokens and lightweight synchronization checkpoints) is strictly protected using industry-standard authenticated encryption (AES-256-GCM).

4. Strict Limits on Google User Data (Limited Use)

In accordance with Google's Limited Use requirements:

  • No Sale of User Data: MailMate will never sell, lease, rent, or trade your Google data, email content, or personal identifiers to third parties or data brokers.
  • No Advertising or Retargeting: We do not use, transfer, or analyze your email content or personal data to serve advertisements, display targeted ads, or build user marketing profiles.
  • No Generalized AI Model Training: We do not use your email data or Google API data to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve generalized or foundation machine learning or AI models. Any AI interaction (such as summarizing an email via Kyle) processes the text solely to provide the immediate response requested by you.
  • No Human Inspection: No employee, contractor, or human at MailMate reads your emails. Human access is strictly prohibited unless: (a) you have provided explicit, affirmative written consent for technical support; (b) required to investigate security anomalies; or (c) required by applicable law.

5. Privacy Gate & Sensitive Data Protection

MailMate includes an automated, local Privacy Gate that inspects email metadata for sensitive contents (including one-time passwords, financial account numbers, tax identifiers, or health records). Emails flagged as containing sensitive personal credentials are automatically shielded and prevented from being dispatched to cloud AI endpoints.

6. Data Retention, Disconnection & Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • Instant Logout: Clicking "Logout" in MailMate immediately terminates your active session and clears in-memory mailbox snapshots.
  • Google Account Permissions: You can revoke MailMate's access to your Google account at any time directly through Google Third-Party Account Permissions.
  • Complete Deletion Request: If you wish to delete all associated synchronization metadata, encrypted tokens, or cache records, you can submit a deletion request to support@mailmate.app or click disconnect in your settings. All encrypted tokens and derived metadata will be deleted within 48 hours.

7. Security Standards & Safeguards

MailMate uses technical and organizational measures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, modification, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 encryption. OAuth credentials and tokens are secured via AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our service or applicable legal requirements. The updated policy will always be published with a revised effective date on this page.
