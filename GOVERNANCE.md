## Repository Principles

- Preserve historical attribution.
- Do not rewrite author identities.
- Do not force-push protected `main`.
- Route significant changes through pull requests.
- Discuss repository deletion, privatization, transfers, and major governance
  changes between the organization owners before acting.
- Continue crediting historical contributors even when they are no longer
  active maintainers.
- Treat repository access and authorship as separate concepts.
