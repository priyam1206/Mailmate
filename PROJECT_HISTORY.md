# MailMate Project History

MailMate was originally prototyped during Code2Create 7.0 and continued through substantial post-event development.

Later work includes the Flask application architecture, Gmail workspace, Kyle assistant/runtime and tools, Calendar intelligence, Work Agent, automations, voice and speech support, privacy controls, local and Tailscale-connected compute, synchronization, testing, and UI/runtime stabilization.

Active development is maintained through the MailMateCS organization. Historical authorship and contribution details remain available in Git history.
